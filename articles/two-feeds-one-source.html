Two City Power Feeds, One Source

Victor Romanov · 29 September 2026 · Discussion on LinkedIn

Two city power feeds.
That looks like redundancy.

But in one of my server room projects, both feeds came from the same substation.
Two cables, one source.
One fault at that substation would take out both feeds at the same time.

So the power design had to create a real second source.
In stage two we added a generator and connected it to both ATS units.
A bus-tie switch links the two sides.
Two UPS with their own batteries and bypass carry the racks until the generator takes the load.
Every rack gets two PDUs, A and B, from two separate UPS lines.

The animation shows the scenario this design was built for.

Count power sources, not cables.
